# Document review app

An Arvale Systems application for checking extracted invoice information before approving a record.

The reviewer can compare a source document with its structured fields, correct missing details, confirm the information, approve the record and export JSON. A queue separates documents that need attention from those ready for review.

The portfolio version uses fictional documents and preloaded extraction. Review takes place in the browser; a live AI extraction service and external business systems are not connected.

This project shows the review step that comes after extraction: giving someone enough context to catch a problem and resolve it.
